DEMO DOCUMENT — LOCAL MVP

This document describes data handled by the local YUDY prototype. A production privacy policy must identify its operator, contacts, applicable rules and retention arrangements before release.

Account and authentication data
The prototype stores your email address and a password hash. It stores hashes of refresh tokens, their expiry dates and revocation status to manage sign-in sessions. It does not store your password in readable form. Android stores encrypted session data on your device using Android Keystore.

Consent records
The prototype stores your account id, the document and version you explicitly accept, and the time of acceptance. Earlier consent records are retained when a new version is published.

Planned discovery data
When their features are implemented, profile preferences, questionnaire answers and asset interactions may be processed to display and personalize exploration content. This prototype does not perform real-money transactions or store payment, custody or brokerage account data.

Local infrastructure
The demo uses the local backend database and Redis. Authentication request counts and source IP-derived identifiers are used to limit repeated sign-in attempts. The MVP does not include third-party analytics. No cross-border transfer, retention period or operator contact is represented as established by this demo text.

Your choice
Read the current documents before accepting them. You can leave this flow or sign out without accepting. The demo currently has no self-service account deletion or consent-withdrawal feature; these production processes are not represented as available.
